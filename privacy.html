Auto-Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Auto-Poster is a personal tool run by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data it uses.

Data accessed through Google

With the owner's permission, Auto-Poster uses the YouTube permission granted at Google sign-in to:

  • find the signed-in account's channel and the titles and view counts of its videos;
  • upload videos to that channel.

How the data is used

The data is used only to choose a topic for the next video and to upload it. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone for any other purpose.

Third-party AI services

To write each script, the chosen subject and the public titles of videos are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, depending on setup). No Google account password, sign-in token or private account information is sent to them.

Storage

  • The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted in the owner's private GitHub repository. It is never published.
  • A short history of uploaded video titles and links is stored in the same private repository.
  • Generated videos are uploaded to YouTube and are not kept after the upload.

Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties, apart from the AI request described above and the upload to YouTube itself. Data is transferred to Google's YouTube service only to perform the upload the owner asked for.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Auto-Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the stored login from the private repository removes the saved token.
