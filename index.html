Auto-Poster

Auto-Poster is a small personal tool that creates one short video a day about a subject the owner chooses and uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • The owner signs in once with Google and allows access to their own YouTube account.
  • Each day it reads the titles of the owner's own most-viewed videos and public search results for the chosen subject, uses an AI model to pick a topic and write a script, builds a video, and uploads it to the owner's channel.
  • Uploaded videos are labelled as altered or synthetic content, as YouTube requires.

This app is not offered to the public. It is run by its owner for the owner's own channel.
